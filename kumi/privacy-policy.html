Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Kumi (the "Game") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our application during the closed testing phase.

1. Information We Collect

To improve the gaming experience and fix technical issues, we automatically collect certain data:

  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with the game (levels completed, session time, gameplay events).
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (IDFA/AAID), and crash reports.
  • Direct Feedback: Information that you voluntarily provide through surveys or contact forms.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Analyze the performance of the game's "Core Loop."
  • Identify and resolve technical bugs.
  • Optimize level difficulty based on real-time user data.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party analytics tools to process data:

  • Google Analytics / Firebase: For tracking events and user behavior.
  • Unity Analytics: (If applicable) For game engine performance metrics.

4. Data Retention

We will retain your information only for the duration of the Kumi closed test. Once the diagnostic phase is complete and development is "locked," aggregated data may be kept in an anonymous format.

5. Security

The security of your information is important to us, but please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at: contact@further.games
