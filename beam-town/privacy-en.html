Privacy Policy – Beam Town

Effective Date: June 11, 2025

Beam Town (“we”, “our”, or “the game”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information when playing Beam Town, available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

a. Non-personal Information

While using the game, we may collect non-personal information such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Play time and session data
  • In-game progress
  • Gameplay events (e.g., levels completed)

b. Analytics and Advertising

We may use third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage data for purposes like:

  • Showing relevant ads
  • Improving game performance and design

These services may use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) but do not access personal data such as your name, address, or email.

2. Use of Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Enhance gameplay experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Analyze user behavior
  • Serve contextual advertising

3. Data Storage

Data may be stored locally on your device or through third-party services. We do not collect or store sensitive personal information such as real names, precise location, or financial data.

4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not share personal information with third parties. We may share anonymous or aggregated data with analytics and ad services to help improve the game.

5. Children’s Privacy

Beam Town is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us to have it removed.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
